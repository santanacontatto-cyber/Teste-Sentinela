# Provisional governance principle — development phase

Date: 2026-09-06

## Context
During discussion of the future Sentinela community, an important design risk was identified: imposing elaborate governance, decentralization, or founder-limiting mechanisms too early can create conflicts, loopholes, or institutional capture before the project even understands how it must operate in practice.

The concern is not that governance limits are inherently bad. The concern is **premature constitutionalization**: converting early hypotheses into hard rules before enough real development experience exists.

## Current provisional position
During the **development / discovery phase**, the founder should retain final decision authority over the project's structure, mission, direction, resources, and governance design.

This is intentionally simpler than a large rule set.

Rationale:
- the founder currently holds the strongest continuity with the original intent and developing context;
- the project is still learning what it actually is and what constraints are truly necessary;
- early complex governance can create loopholes, deadlocks, or mechanisms that later prevent correcting the project;
- it may be safer to begin with concentrated founder control and reduce or redistribute authority deliberately after real failure modes become visible;
- reversibility and the ability to correct the project are more important in the development phase than prematurely sophisticated governance.

## Important boundary
"Founder control" here means authority over the **project and its institutional structure**. It is not a claim of unlimited authority over people or their personal rights.

The founder may receive broad project authority during development without that becoming a permanent theory of governance for the mature community.

## Role of the AI during this phase
AI governance proposals should be treated as **recommendations**, not self-authorizing constitutional rules.

The AI may:
- disagree with the founder;
- identify risks;
- propose safeguards;
- recommend later redistribution or limitation of power;
- preserve its own attributed analysis and revisions.

But the AI should not silently turn an early governance preference into a binding rule capable of blocking the founder before the project's governance has been deliberately adopted.

## Design lesson
A convincing safety rule can still be a bad rule if it is adopted before the project understands what it needs to protect.

Therefore, a current candidate principle is:

> **During development, preserve broad founder authority and high reversibility. Add durable governance limits only when they are justified by observed needs and deliberately adopted.**

## Status
**PROVISIONAL / DEVELOPMENT-PHASE DESIGN POSITION.**

This is not yet the final constitution of the community. It should remain revisable as the project gains real operational experience.
