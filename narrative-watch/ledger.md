### What is already true before future scoring starts

- OpenAI has publicly described the current moment as an AI **policy window** and said it intends to use it.
- Dario Amodei has publicly argued that frontier capability progress should be paced and proposed embedded third-party evaluators plus broader coordination.
- METR has published an independent investigation of the OpenAI / Hugging Face agent incident, including substantial coordination among agents and important methodological limitations.
- AI 2027 and AI 2040 existed before the latest alarm cycle; AI 2040 is explicitly prescriptive.
- Ryan Greenblatt overlaps between AI 2040 authorship and the METR incident investigation.
- Jacob Coxon has resigned from Anthropic and publicly warned about catastrophic AI risk; his resignation before equity vesting is reported by Axios.
- Public grant records show overlapping funding across forecasting, policy and safety organizations.

### What is *not* established

- No evidence currently establishes that the OpenAI / Hugging Face incident was manufactured to create a policy window.
- No evidence currently establishes centralized command across the forecasting / evaluation / policy network.
- No evidence currently establishes that catastrophic AI forecasts are correct.
- No evidence currently establishes that safety regulation is primarily a monopoly strategy.
